## Variant: Editorial Clean

### Design Stance
Content-first, calm, and trustworthy — designed for users who want to read and understand the calendar, not just use it as a tool. Prioritizes readability, bilingual presentation, and visual hierarchy.

### Key Choices
- **Layout**: Asymmetric two-column (calendar + sidebar), generous whitespace
- **Typography**: Inter for UI, Noto Sans Devanagari for Nepali text; large dual-date display
- **Color**: Light theme, sky-500 primary, amber accent for BS dates, semantic red/amber for holidays/festivals
- **Interaction**: Click any date → slide-down events panel; dual converter (AD↔BS) in sidebar; timezone selector in header

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at**: Reading comfort, bilingual clarity, discoverability of festivals/holidays, print-friendly
- **Weak at**: Information density, power-user speed, keyboard navigation, compact view

### Best For
General consumers, diaspora users, educational contexts, anyone who values understanding the calendar over rapid data entry.
